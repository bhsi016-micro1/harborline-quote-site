Medical offices

Cleaning a medical office between patient days

Exam rooms, waiting areas and restrooms each need their own routine. Wipe high-touch surfaces such as door handles, chair arms and check-in counters with a hospital-grade disinfectant, and let it sit for the contact time on the label.

Keep separate cloths and mop heads for exam rooms and restrooms, and change them for every room. Empty sharps and clinical waste only through the practice's licensed waste service.
